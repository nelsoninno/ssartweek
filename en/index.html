Overview

Territories in Transformation

Territory · Identity · Memory · Sustainability · Future

San Salvador Art Week 2027 brings together artists, galleries, cultural institutions, curators, collectors and creative agents in an experience that celebrates creativity, cultural exchange and regional innovation.

For one week, exhibitions, urban interventions, curatorial programs, professional gatherings and special events will activate the city's landmark spaces. The curatorial theme frames contemporary art, experimental architecture, creative technology and public space as languages to explore the relationship between people and their environment.

The program seeks to strengthen ties between institutions, foster collecting and open new opportunities for international collaboration, positioning San Salvador as an emerging destination within the global cultural circuit.

Program

Featured experiences

01

International exhibitions

Contemporary art by local and international artists, with curatorial programming and conferences, panels and professional gatherings.

02

Urban interventions

Public art and activations across the city: murals, large-format sculptures and tours through the Historic Center.

03

Immersive installations

New technologies, digital media and sensory experiences that expand the limits of the artwork.

04

VIP experiences

International networking for collectors and galleries, with exclusive hospitality and high-profile gatherings.

05

Music, food & lifestyle

Contemporary culture that goes beyond the visual arts and activates the city's nightlife and satellite events.

06

Monumental intervened sculptures

40 life-size horse sculptures intervened by leading artists, installed along the city's main avenues, and 40 giant sea turtle sculptures intervened by artists across the four Surf City venues.

07

Bitcoin Art Expo

At Bitcoin Beach and a hall of the National Palace. An exploration of individual sovereignty, freedom and how technology can free us, in a country where Bitcoin has shaped El Salvador's recent history.

08

Symposiums and workshops

Artistic symposiums and workshops among professionals for the cultural enrichment of El Salvador and the region.

Highlights

Two forces that drive the week

San Salvador Art Week is the umbrella that brings many events together. At its heart are SSACO, the institutional and curatorial core, and MAD EXPO, the special event for materials, architecture and design.

The main event

San Salvador Contemporary Art

The institutional and curatorial core of San Salvador Art Week 2027. It connects artists, galleries, cultural institutions and collectors at a regional and international level, and leads the programming of exhibitions, conferences and professional gatherings.

  • Connects artists, galleries and institutions
  • Leads exhibitions and professional gatherings
  • The center of VIP experiences and networking
  • A visibility platform for El Salvador's art

Special event

Materials · Architecture · Design

The official platform for materials, architecture and design at San Salvador Art Week. It connects innovation, sustainability and creativity through immersive exhibitions, live prototyping, experimental architecture and materials labs. This is where the sculptures and large-scale physical work live.
