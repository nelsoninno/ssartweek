Organizers & partners

Who makes San Salvador Art Week 2027 possible. An alliance between the curatorial institution, the organizing foundation and the design platform shaping the first edition.

Main event

SSACO

San Salvador Contemporary Art is the institutional and curatorial core of the week. It connects artists, galleries, institutions and collectors, and leads the programming of exhibitions, conferences and professional gatherings. It is the center of the VIP experiences and networking of the creative sector.

Lead organizer

Fundación Padilla For The Arts

The organizing foundation, committed to promoting art and culture in El Salvador and the region. Through the MACA Central America network it drives cultural exchange, collecting and new opportunities for the creative community.

macacentroamerica.org →
Special event

MAD EXPO 2027

The official platform for materials, architecture and design within San Salvador Art Week. It integrates manufacturers, architecture studios, universities, designers and innovation leaders through four immersive zones, exhibitions and live prototyping.

Positioning San Salvador on the global cultural map
