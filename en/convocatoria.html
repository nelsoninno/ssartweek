Open call for artists

Central America preselection for the exhibition "Territorialities: Identities in Transit", within San Salvador Art Week 2027. April 14 to 18, 2027.

Download the rules (PDF) → Apply (coming soon)

About the call

Territorialities: Identities in Transit

San Salvador Art Week 2027 opens a call for artists across Central America. Proposals should engage with the curatorial theme "Territoriality. Identities in Transit", understanding territory not only as a border space, but as an elastic space that converses with the region's different sensibilities.

Preselection is carried out through each country's curators and then moves to a second review phase by the regional curatorial team.

Participation

Who can apply and what to submit

WhoArtists and collectives aged 18 or over from Central America.
WorksUp to 5 works per artist or collective, in fine art, sculpture, photography, video or performance.
ThemeProposals must reflect on "Territoriality. Identities in Transit".
Documentation (a single PDF)Artistic CV (max. 2 pages), biography (max. 300 words), proposal statement (max. 500 words), up to 4 photographs of the work, installation needs and the price of the work.
Personal detailsFull name, ID document and contact details (phone, email, address, website and artistic Instagram).
